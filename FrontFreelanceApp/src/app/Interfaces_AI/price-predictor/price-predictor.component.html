How to read these metrics?

  • MAE — Average dollar error. Lower = better.
  • RMSE — Penalises large errors more. Lower = better.
  • R² — Proportion of variance explained (0–1). Closer to 1 = better.
  • Decision Tree — Best for capturing non-linear price patterns.
  • Linear Regression — Fast baseline, assumes linear relationships.
  • Neural Network — Can outperform with more data and tuning.
